Part I — Introduction & Overview

1.1 About This Application

The KP Stock Predictor applies Krishnamurti Paddhati (KP) Astrology to predict intraday stock market movements on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) of India. It generates minute-level buy/sell/neutral signals by analyzing planetary transit positions calculated for Mumbai (NSE location) using the Placidus house system and Lahiri Ayanamsa.

4.3 Ascendant Calculation Formula

Step 1: GMST = 280.46062 + 360.98565 × (JD − 2451545.0)
Step 2: LST = GMST + 72.847° (Mumbai longitude)
Step 3: ε = 23.4393° − 0.0130° × T (obliquity)
Step 4: tan(ASC) = cos(LST) / −(sin(ε)×tan(19.056°) + cos(ε)×sin(LST))
Step 5: Sidereal ASC = Tropical ASC − Lahiri Ayanamsa

Part V — Company Horoscope System

5.1 Natal Chart Logic

Each company's horoscope is derived from its NSE incorporation/registration date. The planetary positions at noon UTC on that date form the company's permanent natal signature.

5.2 Transit-to-Natal Aspects

AspectAngleNatureOrbMarket Meaning
Conjunction0°Strong8°Direct activation of natal promise
Sextile60°Positive8°Opportunity; gentle support
Square90°Challenging8°Tension; forced volatile action
Trine120°Positive8°Harmonious; easy gains
Opposition180°Challenging8°Polarization; reversal risk
Transit Score = Σ (Planet_Weight × Aspect_Multiplier × (1 − Orb/8))
Sentiment: Score > +0.5 → BULLISH | Score < −0.5 → BEARISH | else NEUTRAL
